Cuh Golf Rules:

  • Grab 1 scorecard per game. If more than 4 players, team up!
  • Each player/team selects 1 color "tee". On every hole, pick your tee box before you drive: Safe, Steady, Aggressive or Hero. Shorter routes cut the corner but cross the hazards.
  • The secondary tee for each player/team has a mandatory "chill at the clubhouse" for the duration of the game.
  • Teeing off: Par 4 & 5 = roll 2 dice (Driver). Par 3 = roll 1 die.
  • Advance tee 1 peg per number on your dice roll. Water hazard = +1 stroke and back to where you shot from. Bunker = subtract 1 number from your die roll (ex: roll 3, move 2).
  • After tee off, only roll with 1 die.
  • If you roll more than the distance between your tee & the hole, you move forward to the hole and bounce back the remainder (but you always stop at least 1 peg ahead of where you shot from).
  • Land exactly on the cup and you're in the hole, no putt needed.
  • Tie after 18? The tied players play sudden death: hole 19 uses hole 1, hole 20 uses hole 2, and so on until one player has the low score.
  • When you're on the green, roll 1 die. If even, you made the putt! If odd, count as 1 stroke, continue to roll until even. (1 stroke each odd)
